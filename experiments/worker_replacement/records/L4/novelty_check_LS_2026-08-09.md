## N1 — Properties 1, 2 and 4 are INVARIANT to the ambiguity

- **(1) the response is an allocation decision one agent holds** — the manager allocates under
  either perturbation. Unaffected.
- **(2) the newcomer inherits persistent workflow state** — outstanding tasks, artifacts and
  descriptions written for the predecessor. Unaffected, and it is the property §3 itself calls
  *"the uncontested vertex"*.
- **(4) the change is exogenous** — unaffected, and it never leads alone anyway.

**Three of four properties do not care which perturbation we run.**

## N2 — ★ PROPERTY 3'S NEIGHBOURS ARE CAPABILITY/PROFILE SYSTEMS, WHICH FITS THE COVERAGE READING

This is the property that carries the wedge (*"the wedge inside the conjunction is (2)+(3)"*), and
**every neighbour §3 cites on it is a CAPABILITY or PROFILE router:**

- **FlyRoute** — *"a developer-provided registration description that may be incomplete or
  inaccurate… the initial signal for cold-start routing"*, which it **solves by learning a better
  profile from observation.** §3 calls it *"the closest work on the card channel"*.
- **DRAMA** — *"affinity evaluations consider factors such as agent capabilities, location, and
  current workload"*, distinguished as **a single always-accurate system attribute**, with our
  contrast being *"no unreliable, absent, or competing channel."*

**A registration profile and an affinity capability input are both descriptions of WHAT AN AGENT IS
APPROVED OR SUITED FOR. That is the coverage reading. §4's own scope condition is also written in
coverage terms.**

**So the brief carries THREE signals about its own manipulation and TWO of them point to coverage:**

    §3 novelty       neighbours are capability/profile routers   -> COVERAGE
    §4 scope cond.   "COVERAGE information cannot address…"      -> COVERAGE
    §4 perturbation  "prompt-level METHOD SUBSTITUTION"          -> METHOD

**THIS NARROWS DRIFT 1 RATHER THAN LEAVING IT OPEN.** It is more likely a **stale §4 sentence**
than a live question about what the study is.

**STATED AS THE WEAKER CLAIM IT IS, because the stronger one is available and unsupported:** a
method substitution could be *described* in a capability profile, so FlyRoute and DRAMA are not
strictly incompatible with the method reading. **What is true is that §3's neighbour analysis was
written for a capability channel and would need re-doing under the method reading** — a stale
description of *how a worker works* is a different object from a stale description of *what it is
approved for*, and the nearest neighbours to each are not obviously the same.

**This is evidence for the researcher's decision, not a substitute for it.** §3 was
reviewer-verified against primary sources; **nobody has re-read those sources under the method
reading, and this check does not do so.**

## N3 — ★ THE PHASE'S MAIN PRODUCT IS ABSENT FROM §3 AND §7

**§7 frames phase 1 as DIAGNOSIS — a failure taxonomy per cell** (allocating as if the predecessor
remained; trusting the stale card; reading declarations without acting; never verifying; never
asking). **All five are MANAGER failures observed in runs.**

**The L9 phase produced something structurally different and it appears nowhere: a
CHARACTERISATION of when the setting can measure anything at all.**

    uncovered lie   channel requires  nA >= cap   needs CONTENTION to displace
    covered lie     channel requires  nA <  cap   needs a FREE SLOT to misdirect into

**`nA = cap` is the transition, and which side carries the channel is set by whether the lie points
at a class someone else covers.** It is derived offline, holds across three capacities, and
**explains why the shipped lattice measures exactly zero on a realistic portfolio.**

**That is a result about the SETTING rather than about the manager, and §7's dichotomy — *"failures
found → build a policy; no failures → a capability result with boundary conditions"* — has no slot
for it.** **It is, in the brief's own vocabulary, boundary conditions arrived at WITHOUT the
capability result** — and it is the only thing this phase produced that does not depend on a run.

**Raised, not resolved: whether it is a contribution or scaffolding is the researcher's call**, and
it interacts with the L9 choice, because a paper that reports it is making a different claim from a
paper that uses it to pick a lattice.
